A finance app I actually use — on my phone, every day.

Lucre tracks my real balances, net worth, subscriptions and bills behind a private login. What you're about to open is a full, working copy running on generated fake data — so you can click through everything without seeing anyone's money.

What you can do in the demo

  • See every balance and net worth trending over ~6 months
  • Watch subscription detection catch a new charge and a price hike
  • Browse spending by category; recategorize a transaction
  • Preview the daily digest & alert emails it sends me

Why it's interesting to build

  • Real pipeline: Plaid → SQLite → continuous backup to R2
  • One service: FastAPI rendering server-side HTML + HTMX, no SPA
  • Demo isolation: each visitor gets a throwaway sandbox that can never touch my real data
  • Scoped on purpose: decisions & non-goals written down first
